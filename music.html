Music of Lebanon

From the voice of Fairuz to the sound of the oud, music has always carried Lebanon's stories.

Fairuz

Perhaps the most beloved singer in the Arab world, Fairuz's songs about Lebanon, its villages, and its mornings are still played every day on Lebanese radio.

The Oud

A pear-shaped, fretless string instrument at the heart of Lebanese and wider Arabic music, prized for the deep, resonant sound it gives to both classical and folk pieces.

Dabke

A lively line dance performed at weddings and festivals across Lebanon, danced to fast drumming and the mijwiz, a double-reed wind instrument with a buzzing tone.

Modern Lebanese Artists

A new generation of Lebanese musicians blends traditional instruments with pop, electronic, and hip-hop, carrying the country's sound to audiences far beyond the Middle East.
